FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of AI solutions do you build?
+
We build chatbots, content generation tools, data analysis pipelines, workflow automations, recommendation systems, and custom language model integrations — tailored to your specific business context.
How long does a typical project take?
+
Most projects run between 4–12 weeks depending on scope. We start with a 1-week discovery sprint, then move into rapid build cycles. You'll see working prototypes within the first two weeks.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
+
Not at all. We handle all the technical complexity. You bring the business problem and domain knowledge — we translate that into AI solutions and explain everything in plain language throughout.
Which AI models and frameworks do you use?
+
We're model-agnostic. We work with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and open-source models depending on what's best for your use case, budget, and data privacy requirements.
Is my data kept private and secure?
+
Absolutely. We follow enterprise-grade security practices, sign NDAs before any project begins, and can deploy solutions on-premise or in your own cloud environment if required.
Do you offer ongoing support after launch?
+
Yes — all plans include post-launch monitoring and support. We offer retainer packages for ongoing improvements, model retraining, and feature additions as your needs evolve.
