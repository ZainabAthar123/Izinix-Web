CORE_ENGINE_V4.0 // SCENARIO_01 — CUSTOMER_SUPPORT
Scenario 01 / Before

Every ticket touched by hand, every customer kept waiting.

  • ✕Four hours a day lost triaging a shared inbox nobody owns.
  • ✕First response in 6–12 hours; urgent issues buried under noise.
  • ✕Answers vary by agent and mood — CSAT slides quarter after quarter.
Scenario 01 / After

Answers in seconds. Humans only on the moments that matter.

  • AI triage reads, classifies, and routes every ticket on arrival.
  • 90% of tickets resolved automatically with grounded, on-brand answers.
  • 24/7, multilingual — edge cases escalate with full context attached.
Outcome

Days-sales-outstanding down 38%, with zero invoices missed in twelve months.
