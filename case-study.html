Use case 01 — Customer support

From 12 hours to 40 seconds.

How Vantage turned support from its slowest channel into its most loved one — with an AI agent trained on their own docs, tone and two years of tickets.

Client

Vantage

Industry

eCommerce

Timeline

6 weeks

Services

AI Automation · UI/UX

( 01 ) — The pain point

Support was the bottleneck

Vantage was fielding 1,900 tickets a month with a team of five. Every answer was typed from scratch, every escalation started from zero, and growth only made it worse — each new cohort of customers created tickets faster than the team could hire.

  • ✕12-hour average first response — and climbing
  • ✕38% of tickets were the same six questions
  • ✕Zero coverage on nights and weekends
  • ✕Escalations arrived with no history or context
  • ✕CSAT sliding for three consecutive quarters
( 02 ) — What we made

One agent, three pieces

DLV / 01

Support AI agent

A conversational agent grounded in 1,400 help-centre docs and two years of resolved tickets — answering in Vantage's voice, across email, chat and social.

RAGTone-tuned11 languages
DLV / 02

Agent-assist console

The human side: live conversation summaries, sentiment flags and suggested replies, so escalations start at 80% done instead of zero.

UI/UXDesign systemReal-time
DLV / 03

Learning loop

Every resolution is graded and fed back nightly. Weak answers get flagged, the knowledge base updates itself, and the agent gets sharper every week.

AnalyticsAuto-KBWeekly digest
VANTAGE_SUPPORT_AGENT // PIPELINE_V2
  • [ intake ]→Customer message lands — email, chat or social
  • [ triage ]→Intent and sentiment classified in ~300 ms
  • [ retrieve ]→Answer grounded in Vantage's docs and past resolutions
  • [ resolve ]→Reply sent — or human hand-off with summary, sentiment and history
  • [ learn ]→Resolution graded and fed back to the knowledge base nightly
( 03 ) — What was achieved

The numbers moved fast

  • 70% of tickets resolved without a human touching them
  • Every hand-off arrives with summary, sentiment and full history
  • 24/7 coverage in 11 languages, in Vantage's own tone
  • Shipped in six weeks, behind a flag, measured from day one

( 04 ) — Impact

In one quarter, Vantage went from apologising for wait times to being complimented on them. Support stopped being a cost centre and became the fastest, most consistent voice of the brand — awake around the clock, in every language their customers speak.
